## Fixed Manual Scoring Rubrics

All human scores must use integers 1 to 5.

Geometry:

- 1: broken or unusable geometry.
- 2: recognizable but major holes or duplicate surfaces.
- 3: mostly complete with visible artifacts.
- 4: complete and stable from most views.
- 5: clean, stable, and report-ready.

Texture:

- 1: missing or severely polluted texture.
- 2: recognizable texture with major artifacts.
- 3: acceptable texture with visible seams or blur.
- 4: good texture with minor artifacts.
- 5: clean and consistent texture.

Fusion readiness:

- 1: cannot be placed in scene.
- 2: severe scale, orientation, or mesh problems.
- 3: usable after manual cleanup.
- 4: fits scene with minor tuning.
- 5: ready for final render.

Fusion/video stability:

- 1: unstable or visually broken.
- 2: large floating, contact, or lighting artifacts.
- 3: acceptable but visibly imperfect.
- 4: stable with minor artifacts.
- 5: final report quality.
